Why build this integration in code?

This app connects to a vendor as a service, without asking each reviewer to sign in to that vendor. It uses OAuth client credentials: the backend exchanges its own credentials for a short-lived access token.

This demo: direct backend integration

Reviewer→App backend→Risk vendor

Our backend handles the token exchange, renewal and vendor calls. The browser gets the risk result, not the vendor credentials.

Power Apps: an intermediary approach

Power Apps→Custom connector→Intermediary backend→Vendor

Microsoft documents that custom connectors do not support this OAuth grant directly. An intermediary, such as an Azure Function, can perform the exchange.

The tradeoff: direct control over the integration, with responsibility for its security, reliability and maintenance. Power Platform can support the scenario with a workaround; this is not an exclusive capability.
